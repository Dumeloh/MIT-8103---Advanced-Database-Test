Experiment 2: Replication Lag and Stale Reads

Setup:
PostgreSQL 16 primary and replica in an isolated temporary Docker lab.
Replication mode: asynchronous streaming.

Results:
- Primary and replica were confirmed connected in streaming mode.
- Test marker: exp2-test-20261029
- The marker was visible on the replica after the primary insert.
- Primary WAL position at measurement: 0/F027C60
- Replica replay position: 0/F027C60
- Measured replay gap: 0 bytes

Conclusion:
The replica had caught up at the time of measurement. No stale-read interval was directly observed, so this experiment does not establish the duration or frequency of stale reads. Asynchronous replication can still permit a recently committed write to be temporarily absent from a replica.

Limitation:
A controlled lagging interval was not captured. The measured WAL gap is a point-in-time measurement, not a time-based replication-lag measurement.
