## 1. Academic Textbooks & Peer-Reviewed Literature
1. **Silberschatz, A., Korth, H. F., & Sudarshan, S.** (2020). *Database System Concepts* (7th ed.). McGraw-Hill Education.  
   *(Referenced for: Relational algebra, serializability theory, strict two-phase locking (S2PL), multi-version concurrency control (MVCC), and distributed query processing).*
2. **Elmasri, R., & Navathe, S. B.** (2016). *Fundamentals of Database Systems* (7th ed.). Pearson.  
   *(Referenced for: Functional dependency theory, 1NF–BCNF normalization proofs, and relational calculus).*
3. **Kleppmann, M.** (2017). *Designing Data-Intensive Applications: The Big Ideas Behind Reliable, Scalable, and Maintainable Systems*. O'Reilly Media.  
   *(Referenced for: Replication lag anomalies, read-after-write consistency, write skew, split-brain failure modes, and CAP theorem trade-offs).*
4. **Berenson, H., Bernstein, P., Gray, J., Melton, J., O'Neil, E., & O'Neil, P.** (1995). *A critique of ANSI SQL isolation levels*. ACM SIGMOD Record, 24(2), 1-10.  
   *(Referenced for: Formal definition of ANSI anomalies: dirty reads, non-repeatable reads, lost updates, and phantom reads).*

   *(Referenced for: Tokenization standards and restriction of unencrypted storage of Primary Account Numbers).*
